# *Hello, Codeforces!*

We are excited to invite you to participate in [Codeforces Round 1115 (Div. 2)](<https://codeforces.com/contest/2252>), which will take place on [Aug/06/2026 17:35 (Moscow time)](<https://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?day=6&month=8&year=2026&hour=17&min=35&sec=0&p1=166>).

The round will be **rated for all participants with a rating below 2100**. Participants from Division 1 are warmly welcome to participate unofficially.

You will be given **6 problems** and **2 hours** to solve them.

The round was prepared by [TomitaMatei](<https://codeforces.com/profile/TomitaMatei>) and me, [Bufu](<https://codeforces.com/profile/Bufu>).
