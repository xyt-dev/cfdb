## 912B - New Year's Eve

If *k* = 1, the answer is *n*.

Otherwise, let's take a look at the most significant bit of answer and denote it by *p* (with the 0-th bit being the least possible). It must be the same as the most significant bit of *n*. This means the answer cannot exceed 2^*p* + 1^ - 1.

Consider the numbers 2^*p*^ and 2^*p*^ - 1. Obviously, they both do not exceed *n*. At the same time, their xor is 2^*p* + 1^ - 1. Hence, the maximum answer can always be obtained if *k* > 1.
